# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, discuss ideas
rather than people, welcome differing experience levels, and protect other people's privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, and publication of private credentials or personal data are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
security reporting channel. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and share details
only as needed to review the report.
